## June 9, 2026
Original code with two years of changes and improvements first published to a proper
repository, [n6ol-powerline-qrm-monitor](https://github.com/spatula75/n6ol-powerline-qrm-monitor),
and assigned the BSD 2-clause open source license.  Officially "open source."

## May 12, 2024
First [gist](https://gist.github.com/spatula75/e6c654262e420aecf85ba7493a92ec31) 
published to GitHub to share to a few members of the Ham Radio community.

## April 27, 2024
Blog entry [Graphing Powerline Noise](https://n6ol.us/2024/04/27/graphing-powerline-noise/) 
first introduces the QRM monitor to the outside world, including screenshots of fancy 
charts.

## April 21, 2024
First successful charts and graphs produced and uploaded to [n6ol.us](https://n6ol.us/noise/).
Automated collection begins.

## April 16, 2024
Inception. First code established locally.

## March 8, 2024
Powerline interference first detected at N6OL.  Intermittency immediately presents a problem.
